Philosophy & Discipline

auto_stories

“Balancing the precision of Rachmaninoff with the architecture of semantic HTML.”

Classically trained from early childhood at the Steinway grand, Angella Merici has performed in celebrated concert halls across Europe, Asia, and North America. She treats the 88 weighted keys as an exacting study in touch, acoustics, and emotional resonance.

Alongside rigorous recital preparation, Angella channels her intellectual curiosity into front-end programming. Currently exploring the structural elegance of semantic HTML5, fluid responsive CSS grid architectures, and rhythmic keyframe choreography, she discovers an astonishing parallel: the flawless execution of a Chopin Scherzo mirrors the clean purity of well-crafted code.
